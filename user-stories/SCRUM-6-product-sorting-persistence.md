# SCRUM-6: Product sorting persistence

## Story Title
Product sorting persistence

## Story ID / Link
- ID: SCRUM-6
- Jira URL: https://sudharanimsc.atlassian.net/browse/SCRUM-6
- Application URL: https://saucedemo.com

## User Story
As a shopper,
I want my selected sort order (for example, Price high to low) to remain applied,
so that I do not have to re-sort every time I navigate back to the products page.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given I am on the products page and select "Price (high to low)"
- When I click into a product and then go back
- Then the sort order I selected is still applied

## Notes
- Priority: Medium
- Work type: Story
- Status: To Do
- Project: AIDemo

## Summary
This story ensures the user’s product sort preference persists across product detail navigation, improving usability and reducing repeated configuration effort.
